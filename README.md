EMBERLINE — a round-based tactical FPS in Ember Yard. Vanguard (you + two allies) attack; five Sentinels hold sites A and B. Plant the charge or eliminate the defence; first to three rounds takes the match. Four guns, a frag grenade, a buy menu with round money, headshots, recoil and spread, crouching, bots that hold routes, see, hear and shoot back, and a bomb to plant and defuse.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Click to deploy, WASD to move, mouse to aim, click to fire, R to reload, 1/2 to swap weapons, G to throw a grenade, hold E to plant the charge inside a site, B to buy between rounds.
